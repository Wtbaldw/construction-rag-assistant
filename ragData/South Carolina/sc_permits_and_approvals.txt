Most construction projects in South Carolina require permits before work begins. Permit requirements depend on project type, location, and scope of work.

Projects commonly requiring permits include new construction, additions, structural repairs, electrical upgrades, plumbing work, HVAC installation, and commercial renovations. Environmental, stormwater, or coastal permits may also be required depending on location.

Permit approval typically involves plan review and inspections throughout construction. Failure to obtain permits can result in fines, work stoppages, or required removal of completed work.
