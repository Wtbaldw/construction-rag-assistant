South Carolina requires licensing for residential and commercial contractors. Residential builders must be licensed through the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission.

Licensing requirements may include experience verification, testing, insurance, and bonding. Licensed contractors are required for most new construction, major remodels, and structural repairs.

Homeowners are encouraged to verify contractor licensing before hiring to ensure compliance with state law and consumer protection standards.
