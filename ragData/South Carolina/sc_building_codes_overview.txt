South Carolina adopts statewide building codes based on the International Code Council (ICC) model codes, with state-specific amendments. These codes apply to residential, commercial, and industrial construction and are enforced by local building departments.

The currently adopted codes include the International Building Code (IBC) for commercial construction, the International Residential Code (IRC) for one- and two-family homes, and additional codes covering plumbing, mechanical, energy efficiency, and fire safety.

Local jurisdictions may adopt stricter requirements, but they cannot adopt weaker standards than the state code. Builders must comply with both state and local regulations and pass inspections at required construction stages.
