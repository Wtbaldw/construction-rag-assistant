County and city governments in South Carolina manage building permits, inspections, and zoning enforcement. Requirements may vary by jurisdiction and may include additional zoning approvals, setback requirements, or floodplain compliance.

Applicants typically submit construction plans, pay permit fees, and schedule inspections through the local building department.
